# Explainable AI for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening in Rural India

## SIH26038 | Smart India Hackathon 2026

An explainable, confidence-aware AI screening workflow for diabetic retinopathy (DR) designed to support screening in resource-constrained and rural healthcare settings.

## Problem Statement

Diabetic retinopathy is a major cause of preventable vision loss. Rural screening programs face challenges including limited ophthalmologist availability, variable fundus image quality, and the need for reliable interpretation of AI predictions.

The proposed system combines image-quality assessment, preprocessing, DR severity grading, explainability, and smart triage into a single screening workflow.

## Proposed Workflow

```text
Fundus Image
     ↓
Image Quality Assessment
     ↓
Preprocessing

     ↓
